# The First Letter

The lighthouse at the tip of the cape had been home to the same man for thirty years.
His name was Saku, though no one in the village ever called him that. To them he was simply "the lighthouse man."
One morning, Saku found a letter in his mailbox. There was no sender's name, and the envelope smelled of the *tide*.
"Who would come all the way out here?" he murmured, and tore it open.

* * *

The letter held a single line.
*Tonight, please make the light blink three times.*
